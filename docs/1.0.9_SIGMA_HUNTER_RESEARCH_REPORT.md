## First-Principles Interpretation

With current data we only have 1m OHLCV and volume. We do not observe:

- market-by-order queue
- iceberg refresh
- aggressive buy/sell flow
- order cancellation/stacking
- true footprint delta

Therefore "institution behavior" can only be proxied by:

- prior high/low liquidity pools
- session high/low sweeps
- open range sweeps
- volume spikes
- profile voids
- distance to VAH/VAL/POC/LVN/HVN

The research says prior/session liquidity sweeps are real phenomena, but after costs they are not yet a high-quality trading system.

## Practical Rule

Do not promote any of these to large live size.

Acceptable next use:

- paper/shadow only, or
- 1 MNQ test size, with daily stop, while comparing live fills vs replay.

Blocking requirement before live scale:

- Edge Validation must show `PASS`, not `CAUTION`.
- total loss must be less than PNL, preferably less than 70% of PNL.
- walk-forward thirds must all be positive.
- live replay must match backtest behavior.
